Time-to-Temp shows an estimate of how long it will take your home to reach
the target temperature after you change it. When you set a new target
temperature, the thermostat display shows a message such as "IN 20 MINS",
counting down the duration of time until the home reaches the new set point.
The estimate updates while the heating or cooling system runs, so during the
temperature change the user can see when the desired temperature is
anticipated to be reached.
